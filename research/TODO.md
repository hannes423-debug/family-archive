## 3. Resolve Amanda Serafiina's maiden name

**HUMAN — needs a decision, not a search.** The photograph filename says
*Sipovaara*; the gravestone in the photograph plainly reads **"AMANDA SEREFIA
o.s. PERÄLÄ"**. Both are real Kemijärvi farm names, but from different villages
(Sipovaara is Storby/Isokylä; the Kaakkurivaara cluster is Öfverby/Ylikylä).

Possibilities: a remarriage; a birth farm versus a farm she lived on; or simply
a mistake in the filename. Note the stone also spells her *Serefia*, not
*Serafiina*. Ask whoever named the file where the name came from.

## 4. Test hypothesis H4 — the Autioniemi–Talvensaari link

**AGENT.** The 1930 death notice puts *Anselm ja Liisa Talvensaari* among Matti
Autioniemi's mourners, and a family photograph is captioned *Briitta Liisa
Matintytär Talvensaari 1875–1952* — "Matintytär" meaning daughter of Matti.

The obstacle is real and should not be waved away: Anna Emilia is recorded as
**Matiaksentytär**, daughter of *Matias*. If Liisa's husband was Anselm, Liisa is
not Anna Emilia's mother but her aunt. Unless Anselm's fuller name included
Matias — men of this period routinely carried several given names and were known
by one of them.

Resolve by finding Anna Emilia's 1917 birth record, which names her father.
