# Death notice: Matti Autioniemi, 1930

Source image: `docs/media/matti-autioniemi-1851-1930-kuolinilmoitus.jpg`
(newspaper clipping, held by the family). Transcribed 2026-07-27 by reading the
image; **not machine-OCR'd**, so treat individual letters as reviewable.

## Transcription

> Surulla ilmoitamme, että rakas mieheni ja isämme entinen maanviljelijä
> **Matti Autioniemi**
> kuoli pitkällisen taudin murtamana uskossa turvaten Vapahtajaansa Kemijärven
> Ylikylässä Autioniemen talossa 18 päivänä huhtik. 1930 elettyään 79 vuotta ja
> 15 vuorokautta. Rakkauden ikävällä jäimme häntä kaipaamaan minä, 5 poikaa ja
> 2 tytärtä, 1 vävy, 4 miniää, lastenlapset ja lastenlastenlapset sekä laaja
> sukulais- ja tuttavapiiri.
>
> **Kreeta Autioniemi.** (O.s. Helistekangas)
> Matti, Heikki, Saara, Marja, Alpi, Emilia. (Amerik.)
> Juho, Erkki, Hilja, Kalle, Marja, **Anselm ja Liisa Talvensaari.**
> Eetu ja Kaisa Kantola.
>
> Pyytäkäämme aina valmiit olla sillä tulee meidänkin vuoromme kuolla.

## What it establishes

- Matti Autioniemi died **18 April 1930**, at the Autioniemi house, **Ylikylä,
  Kemijärvi**, "having lived 79 years and 15 days" → **born ≈ 3 April 1851**.
  A stated age this precise is unusually good evidence; it is still a stated
  age, not a birth record.
- He was a farmer (*entinen maanviljelijä* — former farmer, i.e. had handed the
  farm on).
- Widow: **Kreeta Autioniemi née Helistekangas**.
- He left **5 sons and 2 daughters**, 1 son-in-law (*vävy*), 4 daughters-in-law
  (*miniä*), grandchildren and great-grandchildren.
- One daughter, **Emilia**, had emigrated to America (*Amerik.*).
- A **Talvensaari** couple, *Anselm ja Liisa*, are among the mourners.
- A **Kantola** couple, *Eetu ja Kaisa*.

## What it does NOT establish

The name list is not labelled by relationship. Finnish death notices of this
period mix children, their spouses, and married daughters' households in one
run, and the line breaks carry meaning that a clipping this size does not
preserve reliably. **Which of the fifteen names are children and which are
in-laws cannot be read off this document.** Counting says 7 children + 1
son-in-law + 4 daughters-in-law = 12 of the 15.

The reading that *Anselm and Liisa Talvensaari* is a married couple, Liisa being
a daughter of Matti Autioniemi, is the natural one and is the basis of
hypothesis **H4** — but it is a reading, not a fact.
